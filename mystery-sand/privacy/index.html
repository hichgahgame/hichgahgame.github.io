Mystery Sand

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Applies to the Mystery Sand app for Android (package com.hichgahgame.mysterysand)

In short
  • Mystery Sand does not connect to the internet. It does not even have permission to.
  • No accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts or microphone.
  • We (Hichgah) do not collect or receive any information about players.
  • Game progress and settings stay on the device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Who we are

Mystery Sand is a guessing game for children aged about 5 to 10, made by Hichgah ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your child's privacy, contact us at hichgah.game@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no servers, accounts, analytics, crash reporting or ads, and it cannot connect to the internet, so we never receive any information about individual players or their devices.

Information stored on the device

To remember a player's game, Mystery Sand saves a few small values in the app's private storage on the device. Only the app can read them, and they never leave the device through the app.

What is savedWhy
Points, stars and the number of rounds solvedTo show progress
Which objects have been found and which questions answeredFor the "My words" collection, and to offer new questions
Which words have been heard, and in which languagesTo show this in "My words" and the language explorer
Settings: sound, music, vibration, language, relaxed speed, calm motionTo keep the player's choices

This information contains nothing that identifies a person. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled or when its data is cleared in Android settings.

Device backup. If the device owner has turned on Android's backup to their Google account, Android may include these game values in that backup so progress can be restored on a new device. This backup is a Google service controlled by the device owner under Google's Privacy Policy; we have no access to it.

Voices and speech

The game says words aloud (object names in English, Spanish, Persian and French). These are sound files included in the app. The game never listens: it does not use the microphone, and nothing a child says is recorded. Some questions invite children to answer aloud to someone nearby; that conversation stays in the room.

Permissions

Mystery Sand asks for no permissions that need your approval. Its only permission is Android's standard vibration (for gentle feedback), which needs no approval and can be turned off in the game's settings. It has no internet permission.

Children's privacy

Mystery Sand is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not collect personal information from children, so there is nothing for us to review, export or delete. This approach is intended to meet the requirements of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws.

Parents and guardians can contact us at any time at hichgah.game@gmail.com.

Google Play

Mystery Sand is downloaded through Google Play. Google may process information about the download and the device under Google's Privacy Policy. This is outside the app and outside our control.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Mystery Sand changes how information is handled, we will update this policy before that version is released, describe what changes and why, and change the effective date above. The current version of this policy is always available at this address.
