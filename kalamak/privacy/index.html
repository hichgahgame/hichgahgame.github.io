Kalamak

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026 · Applies to the Kalamak app for Android and iPhone (com.hichgahgame.kalamak)

In short
  • No accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses, photos, contacts or location.
  • We (Hichgah) do not collect or receive any personal information, and nothing a child says, types or writes is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Kept words, family words, writing progress and settings stay on the device.
  • The microphone is off until a grown-up switches it on, and speech is recognised on the device itself. Nothing is recorded or kept.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • The only internet use is two optional downloads a grown-up starts: a translation helper (from Google) and a Farsi listening model.

Who we are

Kalamak is a free app that helps children aged about 6 to 13 read and write Farsi, part of Hichgah’s الفبای خانه (Alefba-ye Khaneh) initiative. It is made by Hichgah (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy or your child’s privacy, contact us at hichgah.game@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information from anyone, including children. Kalamak has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and we never receive information about individual users.

Information stored on the device

To remember a child’s words and choices, Kalamak saves some values in the app’s private storage on the device. Only the app can read them, and the app never sends them anywhere.

What is savedWhy
Words the child kept (“My words”)To read them again later
Words a grown-up wrote for the family, with an optional meaning and picture (an emoji)So the child can find them
Words the child said or typed that are not in Kalamak’s list yet, and words a grown-up marked as wrongSo a grown-up can check them in the grown-ups’ corner
Letters written on the letter path, and which tips were already shownTo show progress and not repeat tips
Settings: the child’s everyday language, age group, Farsi guide voice, microphone on or off, helper on or offTo keep the family’s choices

A family word or a child’s own word may contain a name if someone types one in. It stays on the device like everything else. All of this is deleted when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared in the phone’s settings; a grown-up can also remove family words in the grown-ups’ corner.

Device backup. If the device owner has turned on Android backup to their Google account or iCloud backup on iPhone, the phone may include these small values in that backup so they can be restored on a new device. This backup is controlled by the device owner under Google’s or Apple’s privacy policy; we have no access to it. The large optional downloads described below are kept out of backup.

Microphone and speech

  • Off until a grown-up allows it. The microphone button appears only after a grown-up switches it on in the grown-ups’ corner, and the phone then asks for permission to use the microphone (and, on iPhone, speech recognition).
  • On the device only. Kalamak listens only after the child taps the microphone button to say a word, and stops when the word has been heard. Speech is turned into text on the phone itself: the phone’s on-device speech recognition (from Google on Android, Apple on iPhone), or Kalamak’s own Farsi listening model. Kalamak always asks for on-device recognition, and where a phone cannot recognise a language on the device, Kalamak does not listen in that language and suggests typing instead.
  • Nothing is recorded or kept. No audio is saved, and no audio or text is sent to us or to any online service. Only the recognised word is used, as if it had been typed.

Optional downloads (the only internet use)

Kalamak works offline. A grown-up can choose, in the grown-ups’ corner, to add two helpers that need a one-time download. Each download starts only when a grown-up switches it on, and when it is switched off Kalamak offers to delete the download to free space.

  • Translation helper. For words beyond Kalamak’s own list, Kalamak can use Google’s ML Kit translation, which translates on the device. When switched on, the app downloads the language files it needs (about 30 MB per language) from Google’s servers. The words the child looks up are translated on the device and are not sent to Google. To deliver the files, Google receives standard technical information such as the device’s IP address and the app’s name, under Google’s Privacy Policy and the ML Kit terms.
  • Farsi listening. For better recognition of words said in Farsi, Kalamak can download an open-source speech model (Vosk, about 50 MB) from alphacephei.com, its publisher’s website. Like any website, that server receives the device’s IP address when the file is downloaded. Nothing else is sent, and the model then runs on the device.

The Farsi voice

Words in Kalamak’s list are read aloud from recordings included in the app. For other words, Kalamak may ask the phone’s own text-to-speech to say them (the engine chosen in the phone’s settings on Android, Apple’s voices on iPhone). Kalamak only passes it the word to say; that engine is part of the phone and works under its provider’s policy.

Words from other apps

On Android, a grown-up or child can select a word in another app and choose “Kalamak”, or share text to Kalamak, to see it in Farsi. That text is only shown in Kalamak on the device; it is not sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Microphone (and speech recognition on iPhone): asked only after a grown-up switches the microphone on, and used only as described above. It can be turned off in Kalamak or in the phone’s settings at any time.
  • Internet: used only for the optional downloads above. Kalamak does not ask for location, contacts, camera, photos or any other personal information.

Children’s privacy

Kalamak is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and Apple’s App Store rules for apps for kids. We do not collect personal information from children, so there is nothing for us to review, export or delete. Features that could matter to privacy (the microphone and the downloads) sit behind a grown-ups’ corner that opens with a two-second hold. This approach is intended to meet the requirements of the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws.

Parents and guardians can contact us at any time at hichgah.game@gmail.com.

App stores

Kalamak is downloaded through Google Play or the App Store. Google or Apple may process information about the download and the device under their own privacy policies. This is outside the app and outside our control.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Kalamak changes how information is handled, we will update this policy before that version is released, describe what changes and why, and change the effective date above. The current version of this policy is always available at this address.
