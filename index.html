Why "Hichgah"

hich · nothinggah · moment

Hichgah is the moment of nothingness: the Now, this very second, before the next thing rushes in. A Hichgah game is a small place inside that second, where minutes stop counting and attention comes home to what is right in front of you: the puzzle in your hands, the friend at your side, the breath before the jump.

Our approach

Five things every Hichgah game is made of

Entertainment and education are not opposites. The best play teaches quietly, and the best learning feels like play. We design for both, with a little space left over for you.

  • ✦

    Play

    Joy comes first: bright worlds, friendly characters and the delight of getting it right.

  • ◆

    Learn

    Real thinking and real knowledge: logic, patterns, timing, and how to care for an animal.

  • ❦

    Grow

    Patience, focus and trying again: skills that matter long after the game is closed.

  • ◐

    Reflect

    Room to pause and notice: what worked, what to try next, and how it felt.

  • ○

    Be present

    No daily streaks to protect and no notifications calling you back. The moment is enough.

Our promises

How we make games, and what we will never do

Time spent with a Hichgah game should feel well spent. These promises hold for every game we make.

  • ✓
    Calm by design

    A gentle pace, forgiving mistakes, and no daily streaks or notifications pulling you back.

  • ✓
    Safe for children

    No accounts, no chat with strangers and no in-app purchases.

  • ✓
    Private

    We collect no personal information, and our games play offline.

  • ✓
    Ads only by choice

    Where a game offers reward videos, they are optional, set up for children and never interrupt play.

  • ✓
    Made for everyone

    Color-blind symbols, helper arrows, calmer speeds and right-to-left languages, so more children can play.

  • ✓
    Honest about learning

    We say which skills a game practises, and we never promise more than play can give.
