Mystery Sand

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Applies to the Mystery Sand app for Android (package com.hichgahgame.mysterysand)

In short
  • No accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses, photos, location tracking, contacts or microphone.
  • We (Hichgah) do not collect or receive any personal information.
  • Game progress and settings stay on the device.
  • The game can offer optional reward videos from Google AdMob. They play only when the player taps the video button after a round, and they are set up for children: no personalized ads, no advertising ID, family-friendly (G-rated) ads only.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Who we are

Mystery Sand is a guessing game for children aged about 5 to 10, made by Hichgah ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your child's privacy, contact us at hichgah.game@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information from anyone, including children. We have no servers, accounts, analytics or crash reporting, and we never receive information about individual players. The only part of the app that uses the internet is the optional reward video, described below.

Information stored on the device

To remember a player's game, Mystery Sand saves a few small values in the app's private storage on the device. Only the app can read them, and they never leave the device through the app.

What is savedWhy
Points, stars and the number of rounds solvedTo show progress
Which objects have been found and which questions answeredFor the "My words" collection, and to offer new questions
Which words have been heard, and in which languagesTo show this in "My words" and the language explorer
Settings: sound, music, vibration, language, relaxed speed, calm motionTo keep the player's choices
How many reward videos were watched todayTo limit videos (see below)

This information contains nothing that identifies a person. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled or when its data is cleared in Android settings.

Device backup. If the device owner has turned on Android's backup to their Google account, Android may include these game values in that backup so progress can be restored on a new device. This backup is a Google service controlled by the device owner under Google's Privacy Policy; we have no access to it.

Optional reward videos (Google AdMob)

After a round, the game can offer a short video in exchange for a small in-game bonus: the points of that round count twice. These videos are provided by Google AdMob, an ads service that is certified for Google Play's Families program.

  • Always optional. A video plays only when the player taps the video button (a play sign, "×2" and a gem) on the end-of-round card. Videos never interrupt play, points are only a score with nothing to buy or unlock, the game plays the same without them, and there are no banner or pop-up ads.
  • Limited. One video per round, and at most eight videos a day.
  • Set up for children. Every ad request is marked as directed to children under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent, ads are limited to the family-friendly "G" rating, and personalized (interest-based) advertising and remarketing are turned off. The app does not use Android's advertising ID or Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features, and no other ad networks are used.

To deliver and measure these videos and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the app automatically sends some technical information to Google: the device's IP address (which can indicate its general location), interactions with the ads (for example, that a video was shown or tapped), diagnostic and performance information, and a device identifier that is specific to apps from the same developer (the "app set ID"). It is encrypted in transit. While the game is open, the app may contact Google in the background so that a video is ready when the player wants one. Google processes this information under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information from Google, only totals such as how many videos were shown.

Parents who prefer no videos at all can simply not tap the video button; playing the game while the device is offline also prevents any contact with Google.

Voices and speech

The game says words aloud (object names in English, Spanish, Persian and French). These are sound files included in the app. The game never listens: it does not use the microphone, and nothing a child says is recorded. Some questions invite children to answer aloud to someone nearby; that conversation stays in the room.

Permissions

Mystery Sand does not ask for any permissions that need your approval. It can use the internet and check the network connection, only to load the optional reward videos. Vibration feedback uses Android's standard vibration, which needs no approval and can be turned off in the game's settings.

Children's privacy

Mystery Sand is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not collect personal information from children, so there is nothing for us to review, export or delete. The optional reward videos are provided by a Families-certified ads service configured for children, as described above. This approach is intended to meet the requirements of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws.

Parents and guardians can contact us at any time at hichgah.game@gmail.com. For information Google holds, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play

Mystery Sand is downloaded through Google Play. Google may process information about the download and the device under Google's Privacy Policy. This is outside the app and outside our control.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Mystery Sand changes how information is handled, we will update this policy before that version is released, describe what changes and why, and change the effective date above. The current version of this policy is always available at this address.
