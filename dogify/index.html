Dogify

Meet the dogs, learn their care, collect their cards

Dogify is a 3D game for children aged 6 to 12. Spin a dog around to see it from every side, find out about its personality, size, food and care, and collect a card for every breed. Coming soon.

  • Starts with five breeds: Labrador Retriever, German Shepherd, Golden Retriever, Beagle and Poodle
  • Kind, accurate care facts, such as why chocolate is dangerous for dogs
  • Plays offline, with no accounts, ads or purchases
  • English, Persian and Arabic, with full right-to-left support

Skills kids practise

  • 🐾Knowing animals

    Each breed's personality, size, where it comes from and fun facts, told simply and accurately.

  • ❤️Caring and responsibility

    What a dog eats, how much exercise it needs and what keeps it safe, like never sharing chocolate.

  • 🤝Empathy

    Learning that every dog has its own needs helps kids see things from an animal's point of view.

  • 🔄Looking closely

    Spinning a dog in 3D and comparing breeds trains kids to notice shapes, sizes and differences.

  • 📖Reading in their language

    Short card texts in English, Persian or Arabic build reading at the child's own pace.
