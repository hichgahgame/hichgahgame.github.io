Dogify

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Applies to the Dogify app for Android (package com.hichgahgame.dogify)

In short
  • No accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses, photos, location tracking or contacts.
  • We (Hichgah) do not collect or receive any personal information.
  • The app does not use the internet. Its settings stay on the device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Who we are

Dogify is a game about dog breeds for children aged 6 to 12, made by Hichgah ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your child's privacy, contact us at hichgah.game@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information from anyone, including children. We have no servers, accounts or analytics, and we never receive information about individual players. Dogify does not connect to the internet, so nothing leaves the device.

Information stored on the device

Dogify saves one small value in the app's private storage on the device. Only the app can read it, and we never receive it.

What is savedWhy
The language chosen in the gameTo keep the player's choice

This information contains nothing that identifies a person. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled or when its data is cleared in Android settings.

Device backup. If the device owner has turned on Android's backup to their Google account, Android may include this setting in that backup so it can be restored on a new device. This backup is a Google service controlled by the device owner under Google's Privacy Policy; we have no access to it.

Ads

Dogify contains no ads of any kind. If a future version adds ads, we will update this policy before that version is released (see "Changes to this policy" below).

Permissions

Dogify does not ask for any permissions and does not use the internet.

Children's privacy

Dogify is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not collect personal information from children, so there is nothing for us to review, export or delete. This approach is intended to meet the requirements of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws.

Parents and guardians can contact us at any time at hichgah.game@gmail.com.

Google Play

Dogify is downloaded through Google Play. Google may process information about the download and the device under Google's Privacy Policy. This is outside the app and outside our control.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Dogify changes how information is handled, we will update this policy before that version is released, describe what changes and why, and change the effective date above. The current version of this policy is always available at this address.
